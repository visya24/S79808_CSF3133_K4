About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in 1979. It originally provided facilities for students to conduct practical activities and supported research in fisheries and marine science.

In June 1996, the institution became a branch campus known as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). It later developed into Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) in 1999 and was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001. On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM officially became a full-fledged public university and adopted its present name, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT offers academic programmes in various fields, including marine science, fisheries, maritime studies, computer science, engineering, technology and management. Its location near the South China Sea supports its specialisation in marine research, aquatic resources and environmental sustainability. The university continues to develop knowledge and produce graduates who can contribute to society.
