Welcome to Terengganu

Located on the east coast of Peninsular Malaysia, Terengganu is famous for its beautiful tropical islands, crystal-clear waters, traditional culture and delicious local food. Whether you love relaxing on the beach or exploring nature, Terengganu has something for everyone.
