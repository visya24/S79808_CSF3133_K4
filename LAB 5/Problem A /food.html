About the Festival

Welcome to the Taste of Terengganu Food Festival! This special event celebrates the rich food culture and traditional flavours of Terengganu. Visitors can enjoy a variety of delicious local dishes, traditional desserts and refreshing drinks.

The festival brings together local food vendors and visitors in one exciting location. It is a wonderful opportunity to discover authentic Terengganu cuisine, support local businesses and experience Malaysian food culture.

Free entry! Everyone is welcome.
